Sera Time collects account information such as email address and name, worker profile information such as skills, experience, profile photo and identity selfie, job/task content, uploaded files, wallet and transaction records, notifications, and technical information needed to operate the service.

The Android app uses Android `FLAG_SECURE` while protected submission previews are open, but no technology can prevent recording with an external camera or another device.
